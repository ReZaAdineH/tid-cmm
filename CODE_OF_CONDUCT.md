# Code of conduct

## The short version

Argue with the model, not with the person.

Assume the other party has seen a different set of organisations than you have, because they
probably have. A disagreement about whether a level descriptor is realistic is the most
valuable contribution this project receives — treat it as a gift rather than an attack.

## Expected behaviour

- Criticise ideas, descriptors, weights and code. Support the criticism with what you have
  observed.
- Accept that your experience is a sample, not the population.
- Be specific. "This is wrong" helps nobody; "this is wrong because in a regulated bank the
  evidence named here does not exist" is actionable.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, or persistent
disruption after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **hello@tid-cmm.com**. Maintainers may edit, hide or reject contributions
that breach this policy, and may block repeat offenders.
