# TID-CMM Community Code of Conduct

TID-CMM welcomes rigorous technical disagreement. The community should be a place where assumptions, scoring logic, evidence requirements and implementation claims can be challenged without hostility.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific.
- Challenge claims with evidence or reasoning.
- Distinguish observation, opinion and verified fact.
- Give appropriate attribution to external work.
- Protect confidential, customer and production-sensitive information.
- Avoid vendor spam, recruitment spam and unrelated promotion.
- Do not use community channels to pressure maintainers into accepting model changes without evidence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, threats, deliberate disclosure of secrets or private data, repeated off-topic disruption, and malicious manipulation of contribution channels are not acceptable.

## Security

Do not publish vulnerability details, secrets, private endpoints or exploit reproduction in a public Issue or Discussion. Follow `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community, project or users.

The objective is not to suppress criticism. Strong criticism of the model is welcome when directed at the model and expressed professionally.
