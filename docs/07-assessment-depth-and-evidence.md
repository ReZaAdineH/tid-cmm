# Assessment Depth and Evidence

TID-CMM uses one model at three assessment depths. The questions do not change; the evidence burden changes.

## Rapid self-assessment

Approximately half a day with a small internal team. It provides a directional baseline, binding constraints, blind techniques and a roadmap. Because evidence is not gathered, C3 prevents the result from being treated as a high-confidence external claim.

## Structured self-assessment

Approximately 2–3 days with domain owners. Scores above 3 are tied to named artefacts. The result can support internal planning, budgeting and leadership reporting when assumptions are clearly stated, but it remains self-declared.

## Evidence-based assessment

Approximately 1–2 weeks. A reviewer who did not originate the score checks the artefacts and validation recency. This is the depth intended for assurance, due diligence, post-incident review and external challenge.

## No certification scheme

TID-CMM does **not** operate a certification scheme. A claim of a “certified TID-CMM level” is not an official TID-CMM designation.

The assessment depth is itself evidence about how far the resulting score can be trusted.
