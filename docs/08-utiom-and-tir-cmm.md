# TID-CMM, UTIOM and TIR-CMM

The ecosystem has a deliberate division of responsibility.

## UTIOM

UTIOM is the **overarching operating model** for security operations. Its seven canonical phases remain:

**Vision → Strategy → Crown Jewels → Threat Visibility → Threat Detection → Response → Continuous Improvement**

Its three canonical pillars remain:

- Leadership & Governance
- Engineering & Enablement
- Operations & Analysis

## TID-CMM

TID-CMM adds measurement depth to the **Engineering & Enablement** side of UTIOM, particularly Threat Visibility and Threat Detection. Its question is:

> **Would we actually see it?**

## TIR-CMM

TIR-CMM adds measurement depth to the **Operations & Analysis** side of UTIOM, particularly Response and Continuous Improvement. Its question is:

> **Could we actually stop it?**

TIR-CMM deliberately contains no detection domain. It consumes detection maturity as an input constraint.

TID-CMM's IR domain therefore measures only the detection-to-response interface. Full containment authority, response engineering, response tempo, recovery and exercising belong to TIR-CMM.

The modules do not fill gaps in UTIOM and are not new UTIOM lifecycle phases. They are deeper measurement instruments for capabilities UTIOM already defines.
