# TID-CMM Community

The TID-CMM community is the practitioners who use the model, question it and share what they learn. There is no membership process and no requirement to complete a formal assessment before taking part.

Canonical site: https://tid-cmm.com

## Choose the easiest way to participate

| What you have | Where it belongs |
| --- | --- |
| A question about applying or interpreting TID-CMM | [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/ReZaAdineH/tid-cmm/discussions) |
| Experience from a real, lab or anonymised assessment | [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/ReZaAdineH/tid-cmm/discussions) |
| An early idea that needs debate | [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/ReZaAdineH/tid-cmm/discussions) |
| A descriptor, weight, constraint or scoring problem | [Model challenge issue](https://github.com/ReZaAdineH/tid-cmm/issues/new/choose) |
| A telemetry, ATT&CK, dataset or crosswalk correction | [Data or mapping issue](https://github.com/ReZaAdineH/tid-cmm/issues/new/choose) |
| A security vulnerability | Follow the private path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |

A contribution does not need to be a pull request. A well-described counter-example or one authoritative correction is already useful.

## Run it, then tell us what happened

The strongest feedback comes from applying the model to a real environment. Start with the [rapid assessment](https://tid-cmm.com/assess), keep the detailed export and sensitive evidence inside your organisation, and share only what has been approved and anonymised.

A short field report can be enough:

1. the environment in broad terms, such as sector and size band;
2. the assessment depth and scope used;
3. the finding that surprised you;
4. what changed because of the result;
5. where the model was unclear, wrong or too demanding.

Do not upload raw assessment exports, customer names, internal architecture, rule content, credentials, indicators from an active incident or anything that could expose an organisation.

## What the community is for

Useful contributions include:

- challenging a level descriptor with a real counter-example;
- challenging a domain or sub-capability weight with evidence;
- identifying an integrity constraint that binds incorrectly or fails to bind when it should;
- improving the telemetry catalogue;
- correcting ATT&CK relationships or derived mappings;
- contributing NIS2, DORA, PCI DSS, CMMC or sector crosswalks;
- sharing anonymised implementation and assessment lessons;
- contributing approved, anonymised aggregate data suitable for future benchmarking;
- improving explanation, documentation, examples and usability.

Agreement is welcome, but evidence that the model is wrong is more valuable.

## How contributions are handled

Questions and early ideas can remain in Discussions while the evidence develops. Scoped changes move to Issues. Changes to scores, level descriptors, weights, constraints or model structure must be reasoned, versioned and recorded in the changelog because they can change assessment results.

Community participation does not silently redefine TID-CMM. Maintainer decisions and the reason for them remain public in the repository wherever disclosure is safe.

## Framework boundary

UTIOM remains the overarching operating model. TID-CMM adds measurement depth to threat visibility and detection. TIR-CMM adds measurement depth to response readiness. Participation in TID-CMM does not require adopting the other models, but contributions should preserve these boundaries.

## Conduct

Be evidence-led, professional and specific. Challenge ideas rather than people. See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) before participating.
